# Thai Market Specifics

## Salary Ranges (2024-2025)

### Software Engineer (Mid-Level, 2-5 years experience)
- **Bangkok-based companies:** 40,000 - 70,000 THB/month
- **Remote/international companies:** 60,000 - 100,000+ THB/month
- **Multinational corporations (MNCs):** 70,000 - 120,000 THB/month

### Factors Affecting Salary
- **Location:** Bangkok pays 20-40% more than provinces
- **Company type:** International/remote > MNC > Thai tech company > Traditional Thai company
- **Tech stack:** Modern stacks (Go, Rust, Kotlin) pay premium over legacy (Java 7, PHP 5)
- **English proficiency:** Fluent English adds 10-20% salary premium
- **Brand recognition:** Agoda, LINE, Grab, Foodpanda experience commands higher offers

### Negotiation Anchors
- **Current salary:** User reported 32,000 - 50,000 THB (range suggests variable/bonus structure)
- **Target range:** 50,000 - 70,000 THB for mid-level roles in Bangkok
- **Walk-away minimum:** Define before negotiation starts (e.g., 45,000 THB)

## Job Platforms

### Primary Platforms
1. **LinkedIn** — Increasingly dominant for tech roles, especially international companies
   - Recruiters actively search LinkedIn for candidates
   - "Open to Work" feature is essential
   - Keyword optimization critical for discoverability

2. **JobsDB Thailand** (jobsdb.com/th) — Largest Thai job board
   - Wide range of companies from startups to enterprises
   - Good for traditional Thai companies and MNCs
   - Requires detailed profile and resume upload

3. **Blognone** (blognone.com) — Thai tech-focused job board
   - Strong community of Thai developers
   - Tech companies prefer this platform
   - Good for startups and mid-size tech companies

### Secondary Platforms
- **Company career pages** — Agoda, LINE, Grab, Foodpanda, True, DTAC
- **Facebook groups** — "Thai Software Engineer Jobs", "Bangkok Tech Jobs"
- **Recruitment agencies** — Robert Walters, Michael Page, Adecco (for senior roles)

## Resume Conventions (Thai Market)

### What to Include
- ✅ Language proficiency (Thai: Native, English: Professional/Limited)
- ✅ Relocation willingness (if applying to Bangkok from provinces)
- ✅ GPA if recent graduate (<3 years experience)
- ✅ Photo for traditional Thai companies (NOT for multinational or international)
- ✅ Age/date of birth for traditional companies (check company culture first)

### What to Exclude
- ❌ Photo for multinational/international companies (bias risk)
- ❌ Marital status, religion (unless specifically requested)
- ❌ High school education (if you have university degree)
- ❌ Salary expectations on resume (discuss in interview)

### Format Preferences
- **Traditional Thai companies:** May expect photo, age, more personal details
- **Multinational/international:** Follow US/UK conventions (no photo, no age)
- **Tech startups:** Modern format, emphasis on projects and GitHub
- **Government/state enterprises:** Formal format, include all education

## LinkedIn Conventions (Thai Market)

### Language
- Profile should be in English (international companies search in English)
- Can include Thai company names in Thai script (e.g., บริษัท กอซอฟท์)
- About section in English for discoverability

### Networking
- Thai tech community is active on LinkedIn
- Connect with: Agoda alumni, Chiang Mai University alumni, Thai tech meetup organizers
- Engage with Thai tech content (AWS Summit Bangkok, tech conferences)

### "Open to Work" Settings
- Enable for: Bangkok, Thailand (Remote)
- Job titles: Software Engineer, Full Stack Developer, Backend Engineer, Platform Engineer
- Visibility: "Recruiters only" (not public green banner)

## Interview Culture (Thai Companies)

### Behavioral Expectations
- **Punctuality:** Arrive 10-15 minutes early (on-time is late)
- **Dress code:** Business casual for tech companies, formal for traditional companies
- **Hierarchy:** Respect seniority, but tech companies are more egalitarian
- **Communication:** Direct but polite, avoid aggressive self-promotion

### Common Interview Questions (Thai Context)
- "Why do you want to leave your current company?" (frame positively — growth, not complaints)
- "What are your salary expectations?" (give range, not single number)
- "Are you willing to work overtime?" (be honest about boundaries)
- "How is your English?" (demonstrate through conversation, not claims)

### Technical Interviews
- **Live coding:** Common for mid-level+ roles (HackerRank, LeetCode-style)
- **System design:** Expected for senior roles (3+ years)
- **Take-home projects:** Common for startups (2-3 day assignments)
- **Pair programming:** Increasingly popular (collaborative problem-solving)

## Target Industries (User Preferences)

### Health Industry
- **Companies:** Bangkok Dusit Medical Services (BDMS), Bumrungrad, Samitivej, MedPark
- **Tech focus:** Electronic health records, telemedicine, medical IoT, HIPAA compliance
- **Salary range:** 50,000 - 80,000 THB for mid-level
- **Pros:** Stable industry, meaningful work, good benefits
- **Cons:** Slower tech adoption, regulatory constraints

### Insurance Industry
- **Companies:** AIA, Allianz, Muang Thai Life, Bangkok Insurance
- **Tech focus:** Claims processing automation, risk modeling, customer portals
- **Salary range:** 55,000 - 85,000 THB for mid-level
- **Pros:** Digital transformation in progress, modern tech stacks emerging
- **Cons:** Legacy systems, bureaucratic processes

### Finance Industry
- **Companies:** SCB, KBank, KTB, TrueMoney, Omise, Sunday Insurance
- **Tech focus:** Payment systems, fraud detection, blockchain, regulatory compliance
- **Salary range:** 60,000 - 100,000 THB for mid-level (highest paying)
- **Pros:** High salaries, cutting-edge tech, career growth
- **Cons:** High pressure, strict security requirements, on-call responsibilities

## Actionable Steps for Thai Job Search

1. **Optimize LinkedIn for recruiter search** (headline, About section, Open to Work)
2. **Create profiles on JobsDB and Blognone** with tailored resumes
3. **Set up job alerts** on LinkedIn for target roles in Bangkok
4. **Research target companies** in health/insurance/finance industries
5. **Network** with Thai tech community (meetups, conferences, alumni networks)
6. **Practice English** if targeting international companies (technical vocabulary)
7. **Prepare salary negotiation** strategy with walk-away minimum
8. **Tailor resume** for each application (mirror job description keywords)
