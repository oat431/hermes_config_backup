# Resume Review Checklist

## Scoring Rubric (100 points total)

### 1. ATS Compatibility (20 points)
- [ ] Single-column layout (no two-column designs)
- [ ] Standard section headings: "Experience", "Education", "Skills", "Contact Information"
- [ ] No tables, graphics, or icons in headers/footers
- [ ] Standard fonts (Arial, Calibri, Garamond) at 10-12pt
- [ ] Saved as PDF from Word/Google Docs (not from design tools)
- [ ] Professional filename: `FirstName_LastName_Role_CV.pdf`
- [ ] Test: Extract PDF text — if jumbled, ATS will reject it

**Scoring:** 20 = all checks pass, 15 = 1-2 minor issues, 10 = 3-4 issues, 5 = major ATS blockers

### 2. Quantification (25 points)
- [ ] Every experience bullet has at least one metric
- [ ] Metrics include: percentages, time saved, users served, revenue impact, team size
- [ ] Metrics are specific (not "improved performance" but "reduced latency by 40%")
- [ ] Scale indicators present (10,000+ users, 99.9% uptime, $50K revenue)
- [ ] Comparisons included (before/after, improvement over baseline)

**Scoring:** 25 = every bullet quantified, 20 = most bullets, 15 = some bullets, 10 = few bullets, 5 = no metrics

### 3. Action Verbs & Impact (20 points)
- [ ] Every bullet starts with strong action verb (Led, Built, Migrated, Reduced, Architected)
- [ ] No "Responsible for" or "Tasked with" language
- [ ] Bullets show impact, not just responsibilities
- [ ] Each bullet follows: [Action] + [What] + [Measurable Outcome]
- [ ] Achievements ranked by impact within each job (most impressive first)

**Scoring:** 20 = all bullets follow pattern, 15 = most bullets, 10 = some bullets, 5 = weak verbs throughout

### 4. Professional Summary (15 points)
- [ ] Present at top of resume (first section after contact info)
- [ ] 40-60 words maximum
- [ ] Opens with role + experience level ("Software Engineer with 3+ years...")
- [ ] Includes 1-2 quantifiable achievements
- [ ] Shows trajectory (past → present → future direction)
- [ ] No personality fluff ("quick learner", "team player", "passionate")
- [ ] Keywords present for target role

**Scoring:** 15 = perfect summary, 12 = good but missing metrics, 8 = generic/personality-focused, 5 = missing or weak

### 5. Structure & Readability (10 points)
- [ ] Reverse chronological order (most recent first)
- [ ] Consistent date formatting (Month Year or MM/YYYY)
- [ ] Company, location, title, dates clearly visible
- [ ] White space adequate (not cramped, not sparse)
- [ ] Bullet points concise (1-2 lines max, not paragraphs)
- [ ] Total length appropriate (1 page for <5 years, 2 pages for 5+ years)

**Scoring:** 10 = excellent structure, 7 = minor issues, 4 = readability problems, 2 = poor structure

### 6. Skills Section (10 points)
- [ ] Categorized by type (Languages, Frameworks, Databases, Tools, Methodologies)
- [ ] Specific technologies listed (not "Backend Development" but "Java Spring Boot, NestJS")
- [ ] Relevant to target role
- [ ] No outdated technologies (unless specifically relevant)
- [ ] Consistent formatting (commas, pipes, or bullets)

**Scoring:** 10 = well-organized and relevant, 7 = minor issues, 4 = vague or disorganized, 2 = missing or irrelevant

## Critical Red Flags (Auto-Fail)

- ❌ Two-column layout (breaks ATS parsing)
- ❌ No contact information or incomplete contact info
- ❌ Missing LinkedIn URL (in 2024+, this is essential)
- ❌ No quantifiable achievements anywhere in resume
- ❌ Objective statement instead of professional summary
- ❌ "References available upon request" (wastes space)
- ❌ High school education listed (unless no college)
- ❌ Photo included (US/UK markets — bias risk; some Asian markets expect it)
- ❌ Typos or grammatical errors (attention to detail test)
- ❌ Unprofessional email address (cutiepie123@gmail.com)

## Market-Specific Adjustments

### Thai Market
- Include language proficiency (Thai: Native, English: Professional/Limited)
- Indicate relocation willingness if applying to Bangkok from provinces
- Can include photo if applying to traditional Thai companies (not multinational)
- Age/date of birth sometimes expected (check company culture)
- GPA important for recent graduates (<3 years experience)

### US/UK/EU Market
- No photo (bias risk)
- No age, marital status, religion (discrimination risk)
- 1-page maximum for <10 years experience
- Strong emphasis on quantification and impact

### International/Remote Companies
- Emphasize English proficiency
- Highlight remote work experience if any
- Include timezone flexibility
- Show async communication skills

## Before/After Rewrite Template

**Weak bullet:**
> "Responsible for developing backend services and improving system performance."

**Strong bullet:**
> "Architected and led migration of legacy Java Struts monolith to Spring Boot 3 microservices, reducing API response time by 40% and cutting deployment time from 2 hours to 15 minutes through containerization and GitOps practices."

**Why the rewrite works:**
- Starts with action verb (Architected)
- Shows scope (led migration)
- Names specific technologies (Java Struts → Spring Boot 3)
- Includes architecture pattern (microservices)
- Quantifies impact (40% response time, 2 hours → 15 min)
- Shows methodology (containerization, GitOps)
