# SSS (ประกันสังคม) Navigation

Verified 2026-11 from SSO guidance sources; rules change — re-verify with 1506 when stakes are high.

## Hospital change (เปลี่ยนสถานพยาบาล)

| When the request is filed | New hospital usable from |
|---|---|
| 1st–15th of the month | 16th of the same month |
| 16th–last working day of the month | 1st of the following month |

- **Annual window:** once per year, 16 Dec – 31 Mar (for the following year).
- **Workplace-move / relocation exception:** mid-year change allowed — file within 30 days of the move (form สปส. 9-02, employer, or app).
- **Channels:** SSO Connect app ("เปลี่ยนโรงพยาบาล"; 2–3 business days to verify), ทางรัฐ app, LINE @ssothai, sso.go.th, or form สปส. 9-02 at any SSO office.
- **Status check:** SSO Connect home card "สิทธิโรงพยาบาลของคุณ" shows the active designated hospital; 1506 or the hospital's SSS desk confirms effective dates.

## Coverage scope

- Treatment + medicine at the designated hospital, **no co-pay** — insulin and chronic meds included; ask for ยาต่อเนื่อง (chronic-med) status and the longest supply allowed per refill.
- Hospital groups run network clinics that also serve their SSS patients (e.g. 30+ clinics for one Bangkok private group) — ask the hospital's SSS desk which nearest location handles routine refills.
- **Emergency (เจ็บป่วยฉุกเฉิน):** go to the nearest hospital regardless of designation — critical emergencies (UCEP) are covered at any hospital for the first 72 hours, then transfer to the designated hospital; notify employer/SSO promptly. A chronic-med refill gap alone is not an emergency — but for insulin-dependent patients, running out PLUS vomiting / deep breathing / confusion / ketones is DKA territory → ER/1669 regardless of scheme.
- Bridge strategy when rights aren't active yet: pay at the hospital with receipts, or ask a pharmacy (ร้านขายยา) to dispense against the previous prescription/box — a bridge refill beats a T1 hitting zero insulin while paperwork waits.
