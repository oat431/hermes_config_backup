# Thai Learner Development Activities: Research Reference

## Core classification

Under the Thai Basic Education Core Curriculum B.E. 2551, `กิจกรรมพัฒนาผู้เรียน` is a separate curriculum component alongside the eight academic learning areas. It has three forms:

1. `กิจกรรมแนะแนว`: Guidance and Counseling Activities
2. `กิจกรรมนักเรียน`: Student Activities
3. `กิจกรรมเพื่อสังคมและสาธารณประโยชน์`: Social and Public Benefit Activities

Do not call it a ninth `กลุ่มสาระการเรียนรู้`.

## Student Activities examples

Student Activities may include `ลูกเสือ-เนตรนารี` (Scouts/Girl Guides), `ยุวกาชาด` (Red Cross Youth), clubs, student organizations, and leadership activities. A school can select and schedule the available activity according to its local curriculum. Scouts/Girl Guides are therefore a national curriculum-aligned activity form, but their exact continuation, timetable, and replacement activity can vary by school.

`ลูกเสือ-เนตรนารี` should be translated as Scouts/Girl Guides. Outdoor and survival skills can occur within Scout programming, but “survival” is not the correct name for the national curriculum component.

## Guidance scope

The official OBEC guidance system frames guidance around educational, career, personal, and social development, with well-being and life skills. A useful progression is:

- ป.1–3: self-awareness, school adjustment, social skills, broad career awareness
- ป.4–6: learning habits, interests, abilities, basic career awareness
- ม.1–3: self-exploration, aptitude and interest exploration, upper-secondary/vocational pathway decisions
- ม.4–6: higher-education planning, career preparation, work transition, personal/social well-being

The presence of three guidance teachers in one school is a staffing pattern, not a national requirement.

## Public-benefit activity

`กิจกรรมเพื่อสังคมและสาธารณประโยชน์` develops service, responsibility, and community participation. It may be scheduled separately or integrated into student activities depending on the school's curriculum structure.

## Evidence and sources

- OBEC curriculum training document: https://academic.obec.go.th/download/obec_book_teacher/basic_education_core_curriculum/051-022_Chut%20Fuek%20oprom%20Laksut.pdf
  - Confirms the three forms of Learner Development Activities and their relationship to the school curriculum.
- OBEC lower-secondary sample curriculum structure: https://academic.obec.go.th/images/document/1512621740.pdf
  - Shows an example with guidance, Scouts/Girl Guides, clubs, and public-benefit activity. Treat the hours as a school-structure example, not a universal national timetable.
- OBEC Guidance Development Group: http://www.guidestudent.obec.go.th/
  - States the guidance vision around educational, career, personal, and social competencies, well-being, life skills, and 21st-century skills.
- OBEC guidance development plan page: http://www.guidestudent.obec.go.th/?p=1071
  - Provides the official guidance-plan document and confirms the OBEC guidance system as the relevant institutional source.
- OBEC curriculum hub: https://academic.obec.go.th/web/news/view/75
  - Lists the Basic Education Core Curriculum and the separate official document `แนวทางการจัดกิจกรรมพัฒนาผู้เรียน ตามหลักสูตรแกนกลางการศึกษาขั้นพื้นฐาน พุทธศักราช 2551`.

## Evidence boundaries

- National framework: three activity forms and their broad purposes.
- School implementation: exact activity choice, weekly schedule, hour distribution, staffing, whether Scouts continue through upper secondary, and which clubs/service projects operate.
- Avoid claiming every school provides Scouts in every grade or that every school has dedicated guidance teachers.
- For a parent-facing BOK, label timetable examples as `ตัวอย่างโครงสร้างของสถานศึกษา`, not as universal requirements.
