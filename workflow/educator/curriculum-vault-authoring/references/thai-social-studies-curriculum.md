# Thai Social Studies Curriculum — Comprehensive Reference

> Source: หลักสูตรแกนกลางการศึกษาขั้นพื้นฐาน พ.ศ. 2551 (Basic Education Core Curriculum B.E. 2551, revised 2560)
> Governing Body: OBEC (สพฐ.) / Ministry of Education

## Quick Facts

- **Subject:** สังคมศึกษา ศาสนา และวัฒนธรรม (Social Studies, Religion, and Culture)
- **Subject Code Prefix:** ส (from สังคมศึกษา)
- **Course Codes:** ส11101–ส16101 (ป.1–6), ส21101–ส23102 (ม.1–3), ส31101–ส33102 (ม.4–6)
- **Levels:** ป.1–ม.6 (12 years), compulsory for all students in all tracks
- **Structure:** 5 strands (สาระ), ~94 concept areas total
- **Model:** Spiral progression — all 5 strands taught every year, deepening across 4 grade bands

**Code breakdown:** ส = Social Studies, first digit = level (1=primary, 2=lower secondary, 3=upper secondary), second digit = grade within level, third digit = stream (1=main), last two digits = course sequence (01–05 per year, reflecting the 5 strands each getting course time).

**Variant note:** At ม.1–ม.6, some schools split into separate courses per strand (e.g., ส21101=Religion, ส21102=Civics, etc.). Standard practice is integrated delivery of all 5 strands within each course.

## The 5 Strands (สาระ)

### 🛐 สาระที่ 1: ศาสนา ศีลธรรม จริยธรรม (Religion, Morality, Ethics) — ~18 concept areas

Buddhism receives primary emphasis as Thailand's state religion, but students learn about Islam, Christianity, Hinduism, and Sikhism.

**Core Doctrines (Buddhism):**
- อริยสัจ 4 (Four Noble Truths): ทุกข์, สมุทัย, นิโรธ, มรรค
- มรรคมีองค์ 8 (Eightfold Path): grouped into ศีล, สมาธิ, ปัญญา
- ขันธ์ 5 (Five Aggregates): รูป, เวทนา, สัญญา, สังขาร, วิญญาณ
- ไตรลักษณ์ (Three Marks): อนิจจัง, ทุกขัง, อนัตตา
- กรรม (Karma): cause and effect across lifetimes
- ปฏิจจสมุปบาท (Dependent Origination): 12-link chain (ม.1–3 intro, ม.4–6 depth)

**Dharma Collections for Daily Life:**
| Collection | Thai | Count | Key Terms |
|---|---|---|---|
| Five Precepts | เบญจศีล-เบญจธรรม | 5+5 | Not killing, stealing, sexual misconduct, lying, intoxicants |
| 38 Blessings | มงคล 38 | 38 | Comprehensive life guidance from basic to enlightenment |
| Sublime States | พรหมวิหาร 4 | 4 | เมตตา, กรุณา, มุทิตา, อุเบกขา |
| Social Harmony | สังคหวัตถุ 4 | 4 | ทาน, ปิยวาจา, อัตถจริยา, สมานัตตตา |
| Paths to Success | อิทธิบาท 4 | 4 | ฉันทะ, วิริยะ, จิตตะ, วิมังสา |
| Householder Virtues | ฆราวาสธรรม 4 | 4 | สัจจะ, ทมะ, ขันติ, จาคะ |
| Causes of Ruin | อบายมุข 6 | 6 | Drinking, nightlife, entertainment, gambling, bad company, laziness |
| Worldly Conditions | โลกธรรม 8 | 8 | Gain/loss, fame/disrepute, praise/blame, happiness/suffering |
| Six Directions | ทิศ 6 | 6 | Duties to parents, teachers, spouse, friends, servants, monks |
| Bases of Merit | บุญกิริยาวัตถุ 10 | 10 | Giving, morality, meditation, etc. |

**Buddhist Holy Days (วันสำคัญ):** มาฆบูชา, วิสาขบูชา, อาสาฬหบูชา, เข้าพรรษา, ออกพรรษา

**Meditation:** สมถกรรมฐาน (40 objects, concentration), วิปัสสนากรรมฐาน (insight), อานาปานสติ (breath mindfulness)

**Scripture:** พระไตรปิฎก structure (พระวินัยปิฎก, พระสุตตันตปิฎก, พระอภิธรรมปิฎก)

**Other Religions in Thailand:**
- **Islam:** 6 Articles of Faith (ศรัทธา 6), 5 Pillars (หลักปฏิบัติ 5), Prophet Muhammad, Quran
- **Christianity:** Jesus Christ, Bible, Catholic vs Protestant, major festivals
- **Hinduism:** Trimurti (Brahma, Vishnu, Shiva), influence on Thai culture
- **Sikhism:** 10 Gurus, Guru Granth Sahib, 5 Ks

### 🏛️ สาระที่ 2: หน้าที่พลเมือง วัฒนธรรม และการดำเนินชีวิตในสังคม (Civics, Culture, and Living in Society) — ~22 concept areas

**Governance & Democracy:**
- Democracy with the King as Head of State (ระบอบประชาธิปไตยอันมีพระมหากษัตริย์ทรงเป็นประมุข)
- Separation of powers: นิติบัญญัติ (legislative), บริหาร (executive), ตุลาการ (judicial)
- Electoral systems and political participation

**Law:**
- Legal hierarchy: Constitution → Organic Laws → Acts → Emergency Decrees → Royal Decrees → Ministerial Regulations → Local Ordinances
- Criminal law: offenses, penalties, procedures, juvenile justice
- Civil law: persons, contracts, property, family, inheritance, consumer protection
- Constitutional law: rights, liberties, constitutional court

**Rights & Duties:**
- 10 constitutional duties of Thai citizens (preserve nation/religion/monarchy, obey laws, pay taxes, education, military service, etc.)
- Human rights: UDHR, children's rights, women's rights

**Government Structure:**
- Central administration: กระทรวง (ministries), ทบวง, กรม
- Provincial administration: จังหวัด → อำเภอ → ตำบล → หมู่บ้าน (appointed)
- Local government: อบจ., เทศบาล, อบต., กทม., เมืองพัทยา (elected)

**Social Institutions & Norms:**
- Social institutions: family, education, religion, government, economy, media
- Social norms: folkways (วิถีประชา), mores (จารีต), laws (กฎหมาย)
- Socialization: agents, direct vs indirect, primary vs secondary

**Thai Culture:**
- 4 regional cultures: เหนือ (Lanna), อีสาน (Lao-Isan), กลาง (Central Thai), ใต้ (Malay-influenced)
- Thai etiquette (มารยาทไทย): wai levels, temple behavior, social conduct
- National identity: ชาติ ศาสนา พระมหากษัตริย์
- Important traditions: สงกรานต์, ลอยกระทง, regional festivals

**ASEAN Studies:**
- 10 member states, 3 pillars (APSC, AEC, ASCC)
- ASEAN Charter (2008), Bangkok Declaration (1967)
- Benefits and challenges for Thailand

**Media Literacy:** Digital citizenship, identifying bias/propaganda, fact-checking (ม.4–6)

### 💰 สาระที่ 3: เศรษฐศาสตร์ (Economics) — ~16 concept areas

**Foundational Concepts:**
- Scarcity, choice, opportunity cost
- Factors of production: land, labor, capital, entrepreneurship
- Supply, demand, market equilibrium, elasticity (ม.4–6)

**Market Structures (ม.4–6):** Perfect competition, monopoly, monopolistic competition, oligopoly

**Sufficiency Economy Philosophy (UNIQUE THAI EMPHASIS):**
- 3 principles (ห่วง): พอประมาณ (moderation), มีเหตุผล (reasonableness), มีภูมิคุ้มกัน (self-immunity)
- 2 conditions (เงื่อนไข): ความรู้ (knowledge), คุณธรรม (ethics)
- New Theory Agriculture: 30:30:30:10 land division
- Application at individual, community, national levels

**Money & Banking:**
- Functions and types of money
- Bank of Thailand: monetary policy, financial stability
- Commercial banks, specialized financial institutions

**Personal Finance:**
- Budgeting, saving vs investing, debt management
- Credit, taxation basics (personal income tax, VAT)
- Insurance, retirement planning (ม.4–6)

**Macroeconomics (ม.4–6):**
- GDP, inflation, unemployment
- Fiscal policy (government spending + taxation)
- Monetary policy (interest rates, reserve requirements, OMO)

**International Economics:**
- Comparative advantage, free trade vs protectionism
- ASEAN Economic Community (AEC)
- Balance of payments, exchange rates (ม.4–6)
- WTO, FTAs, globalization

**Cooperatives:** Cooperative principles (ICA 7 principles), types in Thailand, community enterprises

### 📜 สาระที่ 4: ประวัติศาสตร์ (History) — ~20 concept areas

**Historical Method:**
- 6-step inquiry: define question → gather evidence → evaluate sources (external/internal criticism) → analyze → synthesize → present
- Primary vs secondary sources: ลายลักษณ์อักษร, โบราณวัตถุ, มุขปาฐะ, ทัศนศิลป์

**Thai Historical Periods:**

| Period | Thai | Dates (พ.ศ.) | CE | Duration | Key Features |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Prehistoric | ก่อนประวัติศาสตร์ | Before 1792 | Before ~1249 | Millennia | Ban Chiang, cave paintings |
| Sukhothai | สุโขทัย | 1792–1981 | ~1249–1438 | ~189 yrs | First Thai kingdom, Thai script, Theravada Buddhism, ศิลาจารึกหลักที่ 1 |
| Ayutthaya | อยุธยา | 1893–2310 | 1350–1767 | 417 yrs | Longest kingdom, international trade hub, จตุสดมภ์ governance, two falls to Burma |
| Thonburi | ธนบุรี | 2310–2325 | 1767–1782 | 15 yrs | King Taksin, reunification |
| Rattanakosin | รัตนโกสินทร์ | 2325–present | 1782–present | 240+ yrs | Bangkok capital, modernization (ร.4–5), 1932 revolution, constitutional monarchy |

**Key Monarchs Quick Reference:**

| Monarch | Period | Key Contributions |
|---|---|---|
| พ่อขุนรามคำแหงมหาราช | Sukhothai | Thai script, ศิลาจารึก, paternal governance, kingdom expansion |
| สมเด็จพระนเรศวรมหาราช | Ayutthaya | Declared independence from Burma, expanded territory |
| สมเด็จพระนารายณ์มหาราช | Ayutthaya | "Golden Age": diplomacy with France, Persia; trade and culture |
| สมเด็จพระเจ้าตากสินมหาราช | Thonburi | Reunified Siam after Ayutthaya's fall |
| รัชกาลที่ 1 (พระพุทธยอดฟ้าจุฬาโลก) | Rattanakosin | Founded Bangkok, Chakri dynasty |
| รัชกาลที่ 4 (พระจอมเกล้า) | Rattanakosin | Opening to the West, Bowring Treaty 1855 |
| รัชกาลที่ 5 (พระจุลจอมเกล้า) | Rattanakosin | Abolished slavery, administrative reform (เทศาภิบาล), maintained independence |
| รัชกาลที่ 6 (พระมงกุฎเกล้า) | Rattanakosin | Nationalism, WWI participation, compulsory education |
| รัชกาลที่ 7 (พระปกเกล้า) | Rattanakosin | Last absolute monarch, 1932 Revolution |
| รัชกาลที่ 9 (พระปรมินทรมหาภูมิพล) | Rattanakosin | Longest reign, rural development, Sufficiency Economy |

**World History:**
- Ancient civilizations: Mesopotamia, Egypt, Indus Valley, China, Greece, Rome
- Major events: Age of Exploration, Renaissance, Industrial Revolution, WWI, WWII, Cold War
- Southeast Asian history: Funan, Dvaravati, Srivijaya, Angkor; colonial period; ASEAN formation

**Local History:** Student's province and community; local sites, heroes, oral traditions; methods for researching local history

### 🌏 สาระที่ 5: ภูมิศาสตร์ (Geography) — ~18 concept areas

**Geographic Tools:**
- Maps: elements, types (physical, political, thematic, topographic), scale, grid references
- Globe: continents, oceans, hemispheres, latitude/longitude
- Time zones: 24 zones, GMT/UTC, International Date Line, Thailand GMT+7
- GIS & Remote Sensing: components, data types (vector/raster), GPS, satellite imagery, applications (ม.4–6)

**Physical Geography:**
- Earth's structure, plate tectonics, landforms
- Climate factors, Koppen classification, Thailand's 3 seasons (ร้อน/ฝน/หนาว), monsoons
- Natural resources: renewable, non-renewable, distribution in Thailand
- Natural disasters: earthquakes, tsunamis, floods, landslides, typhoons (2004 tsunami + 2011 floods as Thai case studies)

**Human Geography:**
- Population: distribution, density, demographic transition, migration (push-pull)
- Settlement: rural vs urban, urbanization, city structure models, Bangkok as primate city
- Economic: primary/secondary/tertiary/quaternary sectors, location factors
- Cultural: culture regions, diffusion, Thailand's 4 cultural regions

**Thailand's 6 Geographic Regions:**

| Region | Thai | Prov. | Physical Features | Economy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| North | ภาคเหนือ | 9 | Mountains and valleys; Doi Inthanon | Agriculture, tourism, handicrafts |
| Northeast | ภาคอีสาน | 20 | Khorat Plateau; Mun and Chi rivers | Rice, cassava, sugarcane; labor migration |
| Central | ภาคกลาง | 22 | Chao Phraya basin; fertile plains | Rice bowl, manufacturing, services |
| East | ภาคตะวันออก | 7 | Coastal plains | Eastern Seaboard industry, tourism, fruit |
| West | ภาคตะวันตก | 5 | Tenasserim range, rivers, coast | Agriculture, tourism, mining |
| South | ภาคใต้ | 14 | Peninsula; Andaman and Gulf coasts | Rubber, palm oil, tourism, fishing |

**Environment:** Deforestation, air/water pollution, climate change, conservation, sustainable development, SDGs

## Grade Band Progression (Spiral Model)

### ป.1–3 (Early Primary: Ages 6–9) — Foundation

| Strand | Focus |
|---|---|
| Religion | Simple Buddhist stories (ชาดก), basic etiquette (กราบ/ไหว้), ศีล 5 in child-friendly language, holy days awareness, "sitting quietly" as meditation |
| Civics | Family roles, school rules, simple rights/duties, national symbols, polite behavior (มารยาท) |
| Economics | Needs vs wants, saving (ออมสิน), simple spending decisions, family occupations, basic goods/services |
| History | Self and family timeline, significant people, important days, simple stories about kings |
| Geography | Classroom/school maps, simple directions, weather observation, local environment, seasons |

### ป.4–6 (Upper Primary: Ages 9–12) — Building

| Strand | Focus |
|---|---|
| Religion | Buddha's full biography, core doctrines (อริยสัจ 4, กรรม, อบายมุข 6), พระไตรปิฎก overview, meditation (อานาปานสติ), other religions introduction |
| Civics | Democracy basics, constitutional monarchy, basic laws, local government, Thai culture (4 regions intro), ASEAN member states |
| Economics | Scarcity/choice, supply/demand (basic), factors of production, sufficiency economy intro, cooperatives (basic) |
| History | Thai history periods overview, key monarchs, historical method (basic), timeline skills, local history |
| Geography | Map skills, Thailand's physical features, climate, population, natural resources, environment |

### ม.1–3 (Lower Secondary: Ages 12–15) — Analytical

| Strand | Focus |
|---|---|
| Religion | Full systematic doctrine (ขันธ์ 5, ไตรลักษณ์, ปฏิจจสมุปบาท intro, all dharma collections), meditation practice, other religions in detail, comparative religion intro |
| Civics | Constitutional law, criminal/civil law, government structure, social institutions/norms, ASEAN in depth, human rights |
| Economics | Market mechanisms (supply/demand/equilibrium), economic systems comparison, sufficiency economy depth, money/banking, personal finance, economic indicators, ASEAN economy |
| History | Historical method in depth, detailed Thai history (each kingdom), world history (systematic), Southeast Asian history, historical sources practice |
| Geography | Physical geography of continents, climate systems, natural disasters, population geography, settlement/urbanization, GIS intro, environmental issues |

### ม.4–6 (Upper Secondary: Ages 15–18) — Critical

| Strand | Focus |
|---|---|
| Religion | Advanced Buddhist philosophy, close sutta reading, Buddhism & modernity, comparative religion critical, religion in Thai society, applied ethics, meditation mastery |
| Civics | Political philosophy, constitutional law advanced, Thai political history 1932–present, international law, social theory, international relations, advanced media literacy, civic action |
| Economics | Microeconomics (elasticity, market structures, market failure), macroeconomics (fiscal/monetary depth), international economics (trade theory, BOP, exchange rates), sufficiency as development model, personal finance advanced |
| History | Historiography, critical analysis of Thai historical narratives, comparative history, world history in depth, independent research project |
| Geography | Advanced physical geography (plate tectonics, climatology), advanced human geography, GIS/remote sensing applied, environmental management, geographic fieldwork |

## Strand Interconnections

Social Studies strands form an integrated whole — they should NOT be taught or organized as isolated silos:

| Connection | Description |
|---|---|
| Religion → Civics | Buddhist moral principles form the ethical basis for citizenship (สังคหวัตถุ 4 as social service; ทิศ 6 as social responsibility) |
| Religion → Economics | Sufficiency Economy is grounded in Buddhist principles of moderation (มัชฌิมาปฏิปทา) and contentment (สันโดษ) |
| Religion → History | Buddhism's arrival in Thailand, role in each kingdom, sacred architecture as historical evidence |
| History → Civics | Modern Thai governance evolved through historical processes: absolute monarchy → 1932 → constitutional monarchy → democratic development |
| History → Economics | Thailand's economic trajectory is a historical narrative: agrarian Ayutthaya → modernizing Rattanakosin → NIC → middle-income |
| Geography → Economics | Geographic features determine economic activities: Central Plains = rice, South = rubber and tin, Northeast = plateau agriculture |
| Geography → Civics | Regional geography shapes regional cultures (วัฒนธรรม 4 ภาค); ASEAN is a geographic entity before it is political |
| Geography → Religion | Sacred geography: Buddhist sites define religious regions; Muslim concentration in the South reflects historical geography |

## BOK Creation Pitfalls — Social Studies Specific

- **Don't split Social Studies by grade band for the overview.** It's a spiral curriculum — the same 5 strands recur every year. Use a single comprehensive overview (like Thai Language BOK), with grade-band progression tables inside each strand section.
- **Don't treat Religion as just "world religions."** Buddhism receives ~70% of instructional time at all levels. The BOK should reflect this weighting.
- **Sufficiency Economy is NOT a standalone topic.** It weaves through Economics, Civics (citizenship application), and Religion (Buddhist moderation). Cross-link it across strands.
- **History strands need BOTH chronological narrative AND skills.** Don't just list periods — include the historical method and source evaluation as equal components.
- **Geography is both physical AND human.** Balance both — avoid over-emphasizing physical geography (common in Thai textbooks) at the expense of human geography skills.
- **ASEAN appears in both Civics and Economics.** Coordinate content so Civics covers ASEAN's political/social dimensions while Economics covers AEC/trade dimensions. Avoid duplication.
- **Course codes: ส prefix, not สอ.** Some older references use "สอ" but the official prefix is "ส."
- **The 5 strands should use Arabic numerals (1–5), not Thai numerals (๑–๕), in BOK files for searchability.**
- **Wikilinks between strands are essential.** The curriculum is designed to be integrated — the BOK should reflect this with cross-links. Each strand overview should have a "How This Connects to Others" section with a mermaid diagram.
- **Topic count ~94** across all strands, not ~38 as older/abbreviated references suggest. The full curriculum covers substantially more ground.
