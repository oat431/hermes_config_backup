## Thai Context Elements
- Accounting Professions Act B.E. 2547 (2004) — พ.ร.บ. วิชาชีพบัญชี
- Federation of Accounting Professions (สภาวิชาชีพบัญชี — FAP) — regulatory body
- TFRS (Thai Financial Reporting Standards) = IFRS-aligned since 2011
- CPA exam: 5 papers (financial accounting, tax, law, audit, management)
- Big 4 firms: PwC, Deloitte, EY, KPMG — all have major Bangkok offices
- Revenue Department (กรมสรรพากร) — tax authority
- SET (Stock Exchange of Thailand) — listed company reporting requirements
- BOI (Board of Investment) — tax incentives for promoted activities
- SME tax rates: 0% (first ฿300K), 15% (฿300K–฿3M), 20% (over ฿3M)

## Top Universities
- **Thammasat University** — #1 accounting faculty ("ธรรมศาสตร์ = บัญชี")
- **Chulalongkorn University** — elite prestige, BBA international
- **Ramkhamhaeng University** — open admission, produced countless CPAs

## Key Employers
- Big 4: PwC, Deloitte, EY, KPMG
- Regional firms: BDO, Grant Thornton, Mazars
- Banks: SCB, KBank, BBL, KTB
- SET-listed companies: CP Group, PTT, SCG, ThaiBev

## CPA Exam Pass Rate
~30–50% per sitting — significantly harder than most Thai professional licensing exams
