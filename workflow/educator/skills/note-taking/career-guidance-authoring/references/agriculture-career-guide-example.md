## Thai Context Elements
- King Rama IX's New Theory Agriculture (เกษตรทฤษฎีใหม่) — 30-30-30-10 land division
- Sufficiency Economy Philosophy (ปรัชญาเศรษฐกิจพอเพียง)
- BCG Model (Bio-Circular-Green Economy) — national development model
- Thailand = #1 global exporter of rubber, canned tuna, canned pineapple, cassava
- CP Group = Thailand's largest private company (Fortune Global 500)
- Young Smart Farmer (YSF) government program
- Carbon credits via T-VER (Thailand Voluntary Emission Reduction)
- Kasetsart University has 4 separate agriculture-related faculties

## Key Regulatory Bodies
- Ministry of Agriculture and Cooperatives (กระทรวงเกษตรและสหกรณ์)
- BAAC (Bank for Agriculture and Agricultural Cooperatives — ธ.ก.ส.)
- มกอช. (National Bureau of Agricultural Commodity and Food Standards)
- Depa (Digital Economy Promotion Agency) — AgriTech grants

## Top Employers
- CP Group (Charoen Pokphand) — integrated agri-food, Fortune Global 500
- Thai Union — world's largest canned tuna producer
- Mitr Phol — ASEAN's largest sugar producer
- Betagro — livestock, food
- ThaiBev — beverages, agri supply chain
