# Thai English Curriculum Structure

Source: Basic Education Core Curriculum B.E. 2551 (2008), revised 2560 (2017)

## 4 Learning Strands

| Strand | Thai Name | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| ภาษาเพื่อการสื่อสาร | Language for Communication | Listening, Speaking, Reading, Writing, Grammar, Vocabulary |
| ภาษาและวัฒนธรรม | Language and Culture | Native-speaker cultures, social etiquette, customs, world Englishes |
| ภาษากับกลุ่มสาระอื่น | Language and Other Learning Areas | English across math, science, social studies, arts, health |
| ภาษากับชุมชนและโลก | Language and Community & the World | Careers, ASEAN, online media, exams, lifelong learning |

## Grade Bands

| Level | Hours/Week | Emphasis |
|---|---|---|
| ป.1-3 | 1-2 | Listening & Speaking first, songs, games, basic vocabulary |
| ป.4-6 | 2-3 | Reading & Writing begin, simple conversations |
| ม.1-3 | 3 | Grammar foundations, balanced 4 skills, content-based learning |
| ม.4-6 | 3-4 | Academic English, exam prep (O-NET, A-Level, IELTS, TOEFL, TOEIC), career English |

## Language Choice Rule

**CRITICAL:** When creating English subject BOK overviews, write in ENGLISH — not Thai. The user corrected this explicitly ("lol, you can use English language for English note"). This rule applies to ALL English curriculum content: overviews, topic files, book checklists, everything.

Rule by subject:
- Thai language (ภาษาไทย) → Thai
- English (ภาษาอังกฤษ) → English
- Math-Science → English with Thai terminology as needed
- Social Studies → English with Thai terminology as needed

## Exam Structure

| Test | Level | Content |
|---|---|---|
| O-NET ป.6 | Upper Primary | Listening, Reading, Basic vocabulary and grammar |
| O-NET ม.3 | Lower Secondary | Listening, Reading, Grammar, Vocabulary, Conversation |
| O-NET ม.6 | Upper Secondary | Listening, Reading, Grammar, Vocabulary, Conversation |
| A-Level English | ม.6 (optional) | Advanced reading, writing, grammar for university admission |
| TOEFL | ม.4-6 | Academic English (all 4 skills) |
| IELTS | ม.4-6 | General & Academic English |
| TOEIC | ม.4-6 | Business English (Listening & Reading) |
| CU-TEP | ม.6+ | Chulalongkorn University Test of English Proficiency |
| TU-GET | ม.6+ | Thammasat University General English Test |
