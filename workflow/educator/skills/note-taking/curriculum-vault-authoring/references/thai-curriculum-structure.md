## Key Structural Distinctions

### Foundation Level (ป.1–ม.3) — Spiral Curriculum
- Science is ONE integrated subject (not split into physics/chemistry/biology)
- Math is ONE integrated subject
- Same concepts appear at every grade band at increasing depth
- **Organize by concept area**, not by grade band

### Specialized Level (ม.4–ม.6) — Linear Progression
- Science splits into Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Earth Science
- Math advances through calculus and beyond
- Each year covers different topics
- **Organize by year** (ม.4, ม.5, ม.6)

## International Cross-Reference

| Thai Level | Approx. Equivalent |
|---|---|
| ม.4 | AP intro / IB SL Year 1 / AS-Level |
| ม.5 | AP full / IB SL Year 2 / A2-Level |
| ม.6 | IB HL extension / Further Mathematics A-Level |

Key differences:
- Thai math covers calculus (AP Calc level) for ALL science-math students
- Thai Physics covers AP Physics C scope for science-math track
- Thai Organic Chemistry is introductory vs AP/IB/A-Level depth
- Computer Science is lighter than AP CSA, comparable to AP CSP
