# Thai Career & Technology: Home Economics and Agriculture Research Reference

Use this reference when producing an English-first educational blueprint for Thai learners across ป.1–3, ป.4–6, ม.1–3, and ม.4–6. It is a **research and mapping aid**, not a replacement for the cited curriculum document.

## 1. Curriculum boundary

The Thai Career & Technology area (การงานอาชีพ) is best treated as a standards-based umbrella. The nine applied topics below are useful concept clusters for an Obsidian BOK, but they should not automatically be described as nationally fixed chapters or as being taught in exactly the same year by every school.

Use three labels in future notes:

1. **National-standard alignment:** work process (กระบวนการทำงาน), management (ทักษะการจัดการ), problem solving (การแก้ปัญหา), collaboration (การทำงานร่วมกัน), safe use of materials/tools (การใช้วัสดุ อุปกรณ์ และเครื่องมืออย่างปลอดภัย), resource/environment responsibility (การใช้พลังงาน ทรัพยากร และสิ่งแวดล้อม), and career orientation (การอาชีพ).
2. **Suggested school-unit progression:** a coherent sequence suitable for a school or home-learning note.
3. **Extension / practical knowledge:** health, science, agriculture, food-safety, child-development, consumer, or entrepreneurship knowledge supported by a technical agency or international organization.

Check the exact edition before quoting an indicator such as ง 1.1 or ง 2.1. Do not merge the legacy `การงานอาชีพและเทคโนโลยี` strand layout with later curriculum arrangements without saying which document is being used.

## 2. Reusable progression model

| Band | Developmental emphasis | Evidence of learning |
|---|---|---|
| **ป.1–3** | Self-help, recognition, routines, simple classification, supervised tools, personal safety | Demonstrates a short routine, names materials, follows one-step safety rules, explains a simple benefit |
| **ป.4–6** | Repeatable procedures, measurement, planning, hygiene, teamwork, resource saving, basic quality checks | Produces a usable item/food/crop record, sequences steps, measures, works in a team, reflects on waste and safety |
| **ม.1–3** | Analysis, troubleshooting, management, comparison, documentation, sustainable practice, career awareness | Designs a process, diagnoses a problem, compares alternatives, records inputs/outputs, justifies choices |
| **ม.4–6** | Specialization, quality control, costing, standards, sustainability, entrepreneurship, evidence-based decisions | Builds a portfolio or micro-enterprise plan, calculates cost/yield, uses a standard/checklist, evaluates risk and market/user needs |

This spiral is a **blueprinting model**, not a claim of universal national placement.

## 3. Nine-topic mapping blueprint

### 3.1 Cooking and Nutrition (การประกอบอาหารและโภชนาการ)

- **Alignment:** work process, tool/material safety, planning, resource use, family well-being; cross-link to Health (สุขศึกษา) for nutrients and healthy eating.
- **ป.1–3:** food groups at recognition level (หมู่อาหาร), personal hygiene, washing produce, measuring with household units, assembling a no-cook snack, table manners.
- **ป.4–6:** recipe reading, sequencing, heat methods (วิธีการให้ความร้อน), portioning, balanced meal planning, kitchen organization, simple cost and waste records.
- **ม.1–3:** nutrient functions, menu design for age/activity, sensory evaluation, scaling recipes, food labels, allergens, budgeting, safe storage and leftovers.
- **ม.4–6:** dietary needs and evidence quality, recipe standardization, yield/costing, quality control, menu entrepreneurship, Thai food culture and responsible sourcing.
- **Skills:** handwashing, knife handling under supervision, measuring by mass/volume, mise en place (การเตรียมวัตถุดิบ), cooking, cleaning, recording.
- **Safety/ethics:** prevent cross-contamination; separate raw/cooked foods; control time and temperature; check allergies; avoid unsafe claims or supplements; respect food access, culture, and food waste.
- **Thai terms:** โภชนาการ, สารอาหาร, หมู่อาหาร, สุขาภิบาลอาหาร, การปนเปื้อนข้าม, ฉลากโภชนาการ, พลังงาน, สารก่อภูมิแพ้.
- **Project:** design, prepare, cost, and evaluate a one-day balanced Thai lunch for a specified age group, including a hygiene checklist and waste audit.

### 3.2 Sewing and Clothing (การเย็บผ้าและเครื่องแต่งกาย)

- **Alignment:** materials/tools, process planning, precision, creativity, repair, resource conservation, and possible career/craft pathways.
- **ป.1–3:** identify clothing and fasteners, fold/store clothes, thread a large needle with close supervision, practice running stitch on card/fabric, mend a simple loose button with an adult.
- **ป.4–6:** hand stitches, seam allowance, measuring and marking, fabric grain, pressing, simple repair and a small useful textile item.
- **ม.1–3:** pattern reading, machine-sewing orientation, seam finishes, fit and alteration, fabric properties, care labels, design brief and quality inspection.
- **ม.4–6:** pattern development, production planning, costing, sustainable textiles, digital/market presentation, inclusive sizing, quality assurance, and ethical labor/material sourcing.
- **Skills:** safe measuring/marking, cutting, hand stitching, machine setup only with instruction, pressing, repair, finishing, documentation.
- **Safety/ethics:** needle/scissor storage; machine guards and electrical safety; ergonomic posture; ventilation for adhesives/dyes; avoid cultural appropriation; credit patterns/designers; discuss labor and textile waste.
- **Thai terms:** การเย็บด้น, การเย็บสอย, ตะเข็บ, เผื่อตะเข็บ, เส้นด้ายยืน, เส้นด้ายพุ่ง, แบบตัด, การรีด, ป้ายดูแลรักษา.
- **Project:** repair-and-redesign a worn school or household textile, showing before/after, measurements, stitch samples, material reuse, and quality criteria.

### 3.3 Household Management (การจัดการงานบ้านและครัวเรือน)

- **Alignment:** work process, management, teamwork, resource use, responsibility, consumer awareness, and safe living.
- **ป.1–3:** personal routines, sorting belongings, cleaning a small area, basic laundry/food-area hygiene, asking permission before using equipment.
- **ป.4–6:** task allocation, schedules, cleaning methods by surface, storage, water/electricity saving, simple household budget and cooperation.
- **ม.1–3:** workflow analysis, prioritization, procurement, maintenance, waste separation, consumer information, emergency planning, and conflict-aware teamwork.
- **ม.4–6:** household finance, life-cycle cost, sustainable consumption, service/vendor evaluation, accessibility, digital records, and small home-based enterprise operations.
- **Skills:** plan, sequence, delegate, inspect, budget, compare products, maintain tools, document outcomes.
- **Safety/ethics:** chemical-label literacy; never mix cleaning chemicals; electrical/wet-area safety; safe lifting; non-discriminatory division of household labor; privacy and consent when documenting a family home.
- **Thai terms:** งานบ้าน, การจัดการ, ตารางงาน, งบประมาณครัวเรือน, การบำรุงรักษา, การคัดแยกขยะ, การบริโภคอย่างรับผิดชอบ, ความปลอดภัยในบ้าน.
- **Project:** create and trial a one-week household operations plan with task rotation, resource-use observations, safety checks, and a reflection on fairness.

### 3.4 Child Development (พัฒนาการเด็กและการดูแลเด็ก)

- **Alignment:** family/living skills, responsible care, safety, observation, communication, and ethical decision-making; cross-link to Health and child-development evidence.
- **ป.1–3:** identify basic needs, safe/unsafe situations, respectful interaction, play and reading with younger children only with adult supervision.
- **ป.4–6:** developmental domains (ร่างกาย, อารมณ์จิตใจ, สังคม, สติปัญญา), routines, nutrition/hygiene, play-based learning, observation without labeling.
- **ม.1–3:** age-appropriate tasks, responsive communication, positive guidance, safeguarding, developmental variation, first-response awareness, and referral to trusted adults/professionals.
- **ม.4–6:** nurturing care, inclusive environments, observation records, family/community supports, child rights, safeguarding boundaries, early-years activity design, and care-related careers.
- **Skills:** listen, observe objectively, plan age-appropriate play, prepare safe environments, communicate with caregivers, record and refer concerns.
- **Safety/ethics:** never leave a child unsupervised; no corporal punishment, humiliation, or diagnosis by students; protect identity/photos; obtain consent; respect disability and cultural/family differences; follow safeguarding and referral procedures.
- **Thai terms:** พัฒนาการเด็ก, ปฐมวัย, พัฒนาการ 5 ด้าน, การดูแลแบบตอบสนอง, การเล่น, การคุ้มครองเด็ก, สิทธิเด็ก, การสังเกตพัฒนาการ.
- **Project:** design a supervised, inclusive play-and-routine plan for a hypothetical age group, with hazard assessment, learning objective, caregiver communication, and observation rubric.

### 3.5 Plant Cultivation (การปลูกพืช)

- **Alignment:** work process, planning, tools, resource/environment stewardship, production records, and agricultural career awareness; cross-link to Science.
- **ป.1–3:** identify edible/local plants, plant a seed, water appropriately, observe growth, and follow tool/handwashing rules.
- **ป.4–6:** seed selection, propagation, bed/container preparation, spacing, watering, weeding, compost, growth diary, harvesting, and simple yield comparison.
- **ม.1–3:** plant requirements, soil/media, pests and beneficial organisms, integrated pest management (การจัดการศัตรูพืชแบบผสมผสาน), crop planning, GAP awareness, and input/output records.
- **ม.4–6:** production system design, crop calendars, protected/urban cultivation, soil and water tests, quality standards, postharvest handling, cost/yield analysis, market and climate-risk decisions.
- **Skills:** site assessment, propagation, bed preparation, sowing/transplanting, irrigation, monitoring, harvesting, record keeping.
- **Safety/ethics:** use tools and any pesticide only under trained adult supervision; prefer prevention and non-chemical controls; protect pollinators and water; observe labels and withholding periods; do not eat unverified plants.
- **Thai terms:** การเพาะเมล็ด, การขยายพันธุ์, แปลงปลูก, วัสดุปลูก, ปุ๋ยหมัก, ศัตรูพืช, การจัดการศัตรูพืชแบบผสมผสาน, เกษตรดีที่เหมาะสม (GAP), ผลผลิต.
- **Project:** establish a small school/home vegetable plot with a crop plan, growth diary, water-use record, compost input, harvest/yield table, and reflection.

### 3.6 Animal Husbandry (การเลี้ยงสัตว์)

- **Alignment:** planned work, responsibility, resource use, observation, animal-related careers, and ethical treatment; cross-link to Biology and public health.
- **ป.1–3:** distinguish domestic/farm animals, identify basic needs, observe without teasing, and practice hand hygiene after contact.
- **ป.4–6:** housing, feed/water routines, cleaning, simple behavior/health observations, humane handling with an adult, and basic records.
- **ม.1–3:** species needs, nutrition, breeding basics, disease prevention, biosecurity (ความปลอดภัยทางชีวภาพ), waste management, welfare indicators, and production records.
- **ม.4–6:** ration/yield economics, housing design, vaccination and veterinary roles, traceability, welfare standards, zoonotic risk, enterprise planning, and environmental impacts.
- **Skills:** prepare housing, feed and water safely, observe behavior, clean/disinfect under instruction, record intake/growth, recognize when to seek veterinary help.
- **Safety/ethics:** no unsupervised handling of unfamiliar or sick animals; use PPE as appropriate; quarantine/report disease concerns; prevent zoonoses; provide adequate space, water, nutrition, shelter, and humane care; never treat animals as disposable experimental material.
- **Thai terms:** ปศุสัตว์, อาหารสัตว์, โรงเรือน, สุขาภิบาลสัตว์, ความปลอดภัยทางชีวภาพ, โรคติดต่อจากสัตว์สู่คน, สวัสดิภาพสัตว์, การกักกัน, ผลผลิต.
- **Project:** create a welfare-and-biosecurity audit for a small hypothetical poultry or fish unit, with housing diagram, daily checklist, waste plan, and referral triggers.

### 3.7 Soil and Water Management (การจัดการดินและน้ำ)

- **Alignment:** resource/environment responsibility, planning, measurement, problem solving, sustainable agriculture, and local stewardship; cross-link to Science/Earth and environment.
- **ป.1–3:** observe soil texture/moisture, identify clean/dirty water, water plants without waste, and keep soil covered.
- **ป.4–6:** soil components, organic matter/compost, erosion, drainage, mulching, simple infiltration observation, rain collection, and irrigation scheduling.
- **ม.1–3:** pH/nutrients at introductory level, soil-water-plant relationships, conservation practices, watershed thinking, salinity/erosion risks, and water-quality observations.
- **ม.4–6:** soil testing and interpretation, water budgeting, efficient irrigation, conservation design, climate resilience, remediation limits, monitoring indicators, and cost/environment trade-offs.
- **Skills:** sample/observe safely, measure moisture or pH with a suitable kit, mulch/compost, design runoff controls, schedule irrigation, record water use.
- **Safety/ethics:** never taste unknown water or soil; avoid contaminated sites; use gloves; do not discharge chemicals; respect water rights and downstream users; distinguish a classroom proxy test from a certified laboratory result.
- **Thai terms:** ดินร่วน, ดินเหนียว, ดินทราย, อินทรียวัตถุ, ความเป็นกรด-ด่าง, การชะล้างพังทลาย, การอนุรักษ์ดินและน้ำ, น้ำท่า, การใช้น้ำอย่างมีประสิทธิภาพ.
- **Project:** compare two garden beds with different mulch/irrigation treatments, recording moisture, plant response, water use, erosion signs, and limitations of the experiment.

### 3.8 Food Processing (การแปรรูปอาหาร)

- **Alignment:** work process, preservation, hygiene, quality, resource use, value addition, local products, and entrepreneurship; cross-link to nutrition, chemistry, and food law.
- **ป.1–3:** recognize fresh versus preserved foods, observe drying/pickling demonstrations, practice clean handling and packaging identification.
- **ป.4–6:** simple drying, chilling, salting, or fruit preparation under supervision; clean workspace; date/label a product; compare sensory changes.
- **ม.1–3:** preservation mechanisms, water activity concept, packaging, shelf-life observation, contamination hazards, recipe/process standardization, and basic costing.
- **ม.4–6:** HACCP awareness, process control, traceability, label/regulatory literacy, shelf-life study design, waste/by-product use, product development, costing, and market testing.
- **Skills:** select raw materials, sanitize workspace, measure, process, package, label, record batch/date, inspect quality, and calculate unit cost.
- **Safety/ethics:** control contamination and allergens; use heat/pressure equipment only with trained supervision; never promise shelf life without evidence; follow Thai FDA requirements for foods sold to the public; disclose ingredients honestly.
- **Thai terms:** การถนอมอาหาร, การแปรรูป, การทำแห้ง, การหมัก, การดอง, อายุการเก็บรักษา, การปนเปื้อน, ระบบ HACCP, การสอบย้อนกลับ, ฉลากอาหาร.
- **Project:** develop a small batch of a low-risk local fruit product with a process flowchart, sanitation checklist, batch record, label mock-up, sensory feedback, and cost calculation.

### 3.9 Sufficiency Economy Agriculture (เกษตรตามหลักปรัชญาของเศรษฐกิจพอเพียง)

- **Alignment:** responsible work, resource stewardship, resilience, local livelihood, ethical enterprise, and career planning. Treat it as an application framework across the other agriculture topics, not merely a slogan.
- **ป.1–3:** needs versus wants, sharing, caring for soil/water, using available materials, growing something for household use.
- **ป.4–6:** moderation (พอประมาณ), simple planning, saving inputs, diversity of crops, reuse, household/school food production, and reflection on consumption.
- **ม.1–3:** reasonableness (มีเหตุผล), risk/resilience, knowledge and morality conditions, mixed farming, local resource mapping, records, and cooperative work.
- **ม.4–6:** New Theory (ทฤษฎีใหม่), integrated farm design, water/land allocation, self-reliance versus market participation, value chains, cost-risk scenarios, climate resilience, and transparent enterprise decisions.
- **Skills:** map resources, set a realistic goal, diversify production, budget, keep records, assess risk, work cooperatively, and revise a plan from evidence.
- **Safety/ethics:** sufficiency is not “produce everything alone” or “avoid markets”; avoid romanticizing hardship; respect evidence, land/water limits, labor rights, community knowledge, and fair distribution of benefits.
- **Thai terms:** ปรัชญาของเศรษฐกิจพอเพียง, พอประมาณ, มีเหตุผล, มีภูมิคุ้มกัน, เงื่อนไขความรู้, เงื่อนไขคุณธรรม, เกษตรทฤษฎีใหม่, การพึ่งตนเอง, การกระจายความเสี่ยง.
- **Project:** design a school plot using local constraints and the New Theory logic: map land/water, allocate functions, plan crop diversity, estimate inputs/output, identify risks, and review whether the plan is moderate and resilient.

## 5. Quality-control checklist for future notes

- [ ] Every topic states whether a claim is national-standard alignment, suggested progression, or extension.
- [ ] Exact indicator numbers are checked against the cited curriculum edition and grade.
- [ ] Four grade bands are present without implying that every school uses the same sequence.
- [ ] Each topic contains key concepts, practical skills, safety/ethics, Thai terms, one project, and direct sources.
- [ ] Food, child, animal, pesticide, and equipment guidance is age-appropriate and supervised.
- [ ] “Sufficiency” is explained through moderation, reasonableness, resilience, knowledge, and ethics, not as a simplistic anti-market message.
- [ ] Cross-curricular links are explicit and do not replace the Career & Technology alignment.
- [ ] Direct URLs are retained; a generic agency homepage is not the only citation.
- [ ] Research stops after authoritative evidence is sufficient; repeated search retries are not a substitute for verification.
