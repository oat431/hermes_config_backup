## International Textbook Details

### Physics
- **Giancoli** (Physics: Principles with Applications) — Most popular in Thailand, Thai translation available
- **Halliday Resnick** (Fundamentals of Physics) — More rigorous, used at top tutoring schools
- **Young & Freedman** (University Physics) — For advanced/olympiad students

### Chemistry
- **Zumdahl** (Chemistry: The Molecular Science) — Most popular, clear explanations
- **Chang** (Chemistry) — Close second, widely recommended
- **Petrucci** (General Chemistry) — More mathematical, for advanced students

### Biology
- **Campbell & Reece** (Biology) — THE gold standard, Thai translation available, non-negotiable for กสพท.
- **Raven** (Biology) — Good supplementary, especially for evolution/ecology

### Mathematics
- **Stewart** (Calculus: Early Transcendentals) — Standard calculus textbook
- **Thomas** (Calculus) — Alternative, some prefer the approach
