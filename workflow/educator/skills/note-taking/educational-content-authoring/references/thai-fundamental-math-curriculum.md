# Thai Fundamental Mathematics Curriculum (ป.1-ม.3)

> Source: IPST (สสวท.) Basic Education Core Curriculum B.E. 2551 (2008), revised 2560 (2017)
> 20 concept areas across 3 grade bands | Course codes: ค111-ค116 (primary), ค211-ค213 (lower secondary)

## Curriculum Organization

The fundamental math curriculum uses a **spiral model** — each concept area appears at multiple grade bands, just at deeper levels. Concept-area organization beats grade-band organization for vault structure.

## Grade Bands

| Band | Grades | Cognitive Stage | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lower Primary** | ป.1-3 | Concrete operations | Counting, basic arithmetic, simple shapes |
| **Upper Primary** | ป.4-6 | Abstract thinking begins | Fractions, decimals, formal geometry, data |
| **Lower Secondary** | ม.1-3 | Full abstract reasoning | Algebra, rational numbers, sets, proofs |
