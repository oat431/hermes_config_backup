| Physics | ว301–ว303 | ~23 topics (Mechanics → Astrophysics) |
| Chemistry | ว311–ว313 | ~20 topics (Atomic Structure → Industrial Chemistry) |
| Biology | ว321–ว323 | ~20 topics (Cell Biology → Bioethics) |

| Subject | Codes | Topics |

| Computer Science | ว331–ว333 | ~12 topics (Computational Thinking → Digital Citizenship) |
| Earth Science | ว341 | ~10 topics (Geology, Meteorology, Astronomy) |

## Key Differences from AP/IB/A-Level

- Thai calculus covers AP Calc BC level for ALL science-math students
- Thai Physics covers AP Physics C scope (mechanics + E&M)
- Thai Organic Chemistry is introductory compared to AP/IB depth
- Computer Science is lighter than AP CSA, comparable to AP CSP
- Additional Math extends into IB HL / Further Pure A-Level territory

## Governing Bodies

- **สพฐ. (OBEC)** — Core curriculum (หลักสูตรแกนกลาง)
- **สสวท. (IPST)** — Subject standards for science/math/technology
- **NIETS** — Exam administration (O-NET, A-Level)

## Exam Structure

| Exam | Content |
|---|---|
| O-NET | Core subjects (Thai, Social, English, Math, Science) |
| A-Level Applied | Subject-specific: Math 1, Math 2, Science (Physics/Chemistry/Biology) |
| TPAT | Aptitude tests (Medicine, Engineering, etc.) |
| TGAT | General aptitude (communication, thinking, ethics) |
