# Thai Fundamental Science Curriculum (ป.1-ม.3)

> Source: IPST (สสวท.) Basic Education Core Curriculum B.E. 2551 (2008), revised 2560 (2017)
> 22 concept areas across 4 domains and 3 grade bands | Course codes: ว111-ว116 (primary), ว211-ว213 (lower secondary)

## Curriculum Organization

The fundamental science curriculum uses a **spiral model** — topics reappear across grade bands at increasing depth. Concept-area organization (not grade-band) is the correct vault structure.

## Grade Bands

| Band | Grades | Cognitive Stage | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lower Primary** | ป.1-3 | Concrete operations | Observation, classification, simple investigation |
| **Upper Primary** | ป.4-6 | Abstract thinking begins | Process skills, basic concepts, measurements |
| **Lower Secondary** | ม.1-3 | Full abstract reasoning | Formal laws, equations, systems thinking |
