# Reference: Career Capability BOK and Cross-Framework Roadmap

## Purpose

Use this pattern when a mature technical knowledge vault needs a next-stage learning layer for promotion, senior engineering, staff engineering, technical program management, project management, engineering management, or product management.

The new BOK is an **overlay**. It should connect existing bodies of knowledge rather than copy them.

| DMBOK | How do we manage data as an organizational asset? |
| EMBOK | What knowledge and competencies support engineering management? |

## Recommended Overlay Domains

1. Career map and competency model
2. Problem framing and business value
3. Technical direction and architecture leadership
4. Delivery, program, and execution management
5. People, teams, and engineering leadership
6. Communication, influence, and stakeholder management
7. Quality, security, reliability, and service ownership
8. Finance, commercial, and organizational economics
9. Product, customer, and market context
10. Enterprise, transformation, and systems thinking
11. Applied case studies and professional evidence

## Shared-Core Sequence

1. Problem framing, value, communication, and decision making
2. Technical direction, architecture, quality, security, and operations
3. Delivery, risk, estimation, dependencies, and governance
4. People leadership and organizational context
5. Finance, commercial concerns, product, or enterprise specialization

## Three Status Dimensions

Never collapse these into one coverage percentage:

- **Topic coverage:** the concept exists somewhere in the vault
- **Source-book status:** the canonical or recommended book has been summarized
- **Applied evidence:** the learner used the concept to produce an artifact or achieve a result

## Artifact-First Pattern

Every domain should produce evidence such as:

- Problem statement
- Current-state and future-state description
- Stakeholder map
- Business case and value hypothesis
- Architecture Decision Record
- Trade-study report
- Risk and assumption register
- Dependency map
- Delivery roadmap
- Quality/security/operational-readiness plan
- Incident review
- Product discovery report
- Benefits-realization review
- Retrospective

Reuse existing templates from the user's document_template library. A new BOK name is not a reason to duplicate an existing artifact template.

## Cross-BOK Capstone

A strong capstone combines:

1. Problem and stakeholder context
2. Business objective and expected value
3. Current-state and future-state models
4. System context and architecture options
5. Decision records and trade-offs
6. Scope, roadmap, dependencies, risks, and assumptions
7. Quality, security, reliability, and operational readiness
8. Communication and governance plan
9. Delivery evidence
10. Benefits review and retrospective

The capstone should be connected to SWEBOK, PMBOK, SEBoK, BABOK, CyBOK, and DMBOK through wikilinks.

## Book Checklist Rules

- Search existing vault summaries before adding a recommendation.
- Use one primary source per capability before supplementary books.
- Explain the exact gap each book fills.
- Keep framework references separate from practical books and deep dives.
- A long list is not a learning path.
- Mark source-book status separately from topic coverage and applied evidence.
