# Protein Food Equivalents

Quick-reference table for translating gram targets into real food. Use when giving nutrition guidance to clients who don't track macros.

## Single-Item Reference

| Food | Amount | Protein (g) | Notes |
|------|--------|-------------|-------|
| Chicken breast (cooked) | 1 large (~200g raw) | ~60 | Leanest option |

## Sample Day Assemblies

### 160-170g target
- 1 large chicken breast (60g) + 4 eggs (24g) + 1 scoop whey (25g) + 200g Greek yogurt (20g) + 1 can tuna (25g) = **~154g**
- Add 200g cottage cheese → **~176g**

### 140-150g target
- 1 small chicken breast (35g) + 3 eggs (18g) + 1 scoop whey (25g) + 200g Greek yogurt (20g) + 150g lean beef (40g) = **~138g**

### 120-130g target
- 1 small chicken breast (35g) + 3 eggs (18g) + 1 scoop whey (25g) + 200g cottage cheese (22g) + 1 can tuna (25g) = **~125g**

## Usage Notes

- These are rough — actual protein varies by brand, cut, and cooking method. Good enough for adherence guidance.
- Remaining calories (rice, bread, vegetables, oil, sauces) fill the deficit target after protein is accounted for.
- If the user is vegetarian/vegan, swap to tofu, tempeh, seitan, legumes — note that plant protein typically has lower leucine content per gram.
