# Alternative Exercise Mapping

When building a program for a client who trains in a crowded commercial gym, provide 2-3 alternatives for every exercise. This prevents skipped exercises when equipment is taken.

## Mapping Format

For each exercise in the program:
```
| Planned Exercise | Alt 1 (best match) | Alt 2 | Alt 3 (last resort) |
```

## Exercise Substitution Rules

- **Same movement pattern, same muscle group.** Don't swap a squat for a curl.
- **Alt 1** should be the closest possible match (barbell ↔ dumbbell ↔ Smith machine variant).
- **Alt 2** should use different equipment but same pattern (e.g., machine ↔ cable).
- **Alt 3** is bodyweight or minimal-equipment, for when the gym is chaos.

## "Gym Is Packed" Protocol

When multiple stations are taken and you can't follow the planned session:

### Dumbbell-Only Circuit (3 rounds, 60s rest)
- DB Thrusters (squat + OHP) × 10
- DB Row (each arm) × 10
- DB Romanian Deadlift × 10
- Push-ups × 10
- Plank × 30 sec

### Bodyweight-Only Circuit (4 rounds, 45s rest)
- Push-ups × 12
- Bodyweight Squats × 15
- Walking Lunges × 10/leg
- Inverted Rows × 8
- Dead Bugs × 10/side

## Don't-Do List (include in the note)

- Skip the exercise entirely and go home
- Wait 15+ minutes for a station — swap immediately
- Do an unrelated exercise because it's the only open machine
- Rearrange the whole session (one swap is fine, full reorder isn't)
- If a station is consistently packed, rework the program around available equipment
