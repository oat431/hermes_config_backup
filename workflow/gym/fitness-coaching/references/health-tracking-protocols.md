# Health Tracking Protocols — cadences, targets, and the "how often" answers

Answers for the recurring question pattern "should I measure/track X more often?" Cadence principle: **more frequent ≠ more useful.** Biological half-lives set cadences; weekly averages beat daily noise; free proxies (BP, weight, waist) cover the gaps between expensive draws. Panomete accepts direct answers with biological rationale — decide, don't defer.

## Blood pressure (home cuff)

- **Protocol:** seated 5 min rest, back supported, feet flat, arm at heart level, bare skin, empty bladder, no coffee/exercise 30 min prior. **Two readings 1 min apart — record BOTH + average** (single readings lie).
- **Cuff validity:** upper-arm only. Wrist cuffs under-read → use gym/clinic machine instead.
- **Schedule:** baseline week = every morning (7 days); maintenance = Sun + Thu. Daily forever = noise-chasing (±5–10 mmHg day-to-day with sleep/sodium/training); weekly average of 4 readings is the trend signal.
- **Classification:** <120/80 optimal · 120–129/<80 elevated · 130–139/80–89 stage 1 · ≥140/90 stage 2.
- **Target:** <130/80 trending toward <120/80 with weight loss (~1 mmHg per kg lost).
- **Action rule:** two separate sessions averaging ≥140/90 → daily confirmation week + doctor visit.

## Blood panel (clinic draw)

- **Pre-draw:** fast 8–12 h, water allowed, no alcohol 48 h, no hard training night before. Get printed report WITH reference ranges.
- **Panel:** fasting glucose, HbA1c, total/LDL/HDL/TG, ALT, AST, creatinine, eGFR, uric acid. Optional: vitamin D, hs-CRP.
- **Glossary + targets** (user wants full names + plain meaning; include both in every health template):

| Alias | Full name | Plain meaning | Target |
|-------|-----------|---------------|--------|
| HbA1c | Hemoglobin A1c (glycated hemoglobin) | 3-month average blood sugar | <5.7% |
| LDL | Low-Density Lipoprotein | "bad" cholesterol | <100 mg/dL |
| HDL | High-Density Lipoprotein | "good" cholesterol | ≥40, ideal ≥60 mg/dL |
| Triglycerides | Triglycerides | fat in blood after meals | <150 mg/dL |
| ALT / AST | Alanine / Aspartate Aminotransferase | liver enzymes — fatty liver shows here | in lab range |
| eGFR | estimated Glomerular Filtration Rate | kidney function % | ≥90 mL/min/1.73m² |
| Creatinine | Creatinine | kidney filter test | 0.7–1.3 mg/dL |
| Uric acid | Uric acid | protein-breakdown waste, gout risk | <7.0 mg/dL |
| Fasting glucose | Blood sugar (fasting) | sugar after 8–12 h fast | <100 mg/dL |

- **Retest cadence: 6 months** (weight loss moves every row; monthly = expensive noise — HbA1c is a 3-month average, lipids swing ±5–10% biologically). **Conditional:** any ▲ marker → recheck that marker alone at 3 months (e.g., HbA1c ≥5.7, high uric acid).

## Run/walk frequency gating (121 kg)

- **1 structured run/walk session/week** (Wed). Daily run-walk at this bodyweight = joint risk; dose control first.
- Daily walking is separate and unlimited — lives in the steps column, not the run log.
- Morning walk encouraged (walking, not jogging).
- Scale-up path: 2 sessions → 3–4 sessions only at lower bodyweight AND only after the run log has produced real minutes/distance/HR data. The 4-week empty-log streak (W5–W8) blocks ALL progression decisions — never progress cardio from missing data.

## Body metrics grouping rule

Group tracking actions by the sitting they happen in: scale + tape + photos all happen Sunday morning → ONE file (`body-metrics.md`). When a file's scope outgrows its name, rename to the scope, don't fragment into one-file-per-metric.
