# N-Week Program — Phase N

## Schedule Template

**Default (4-day spread):**

| Day | Focus | Cardio |
|-----|-------|--------|
| Mon | Upper A | — |
| Tue | Lower A | — |
| Wed | Rest / walk | Light walk |
| Thu | Upper B | — |
| Fri | Lower B | — |
| Sat | Run/Walk | Intervals |
| Sun | Rest | — |

**Adapted (clustered days — e.g., Mon/Tue/Wed/Sat):**

When the user has consecutive available days, restructure: the middle day of any 3+ day block becomes light (cardio + accessories). The isolated day becomes Full Body.

| Day | Focus | Load | Time |
|-----|-------|------|------|
| Mon | Upper | Heavy — compounds | ~55 min |
| Tue | Lower | Heavy — squat focus | ~50 min |
| Wed | Run/Walk + Accessories | Light — cardio + arms/core only | ~40 min |
| Thu | Rest | — | — |
| Fri | Rest | — | — |
| Sat | Full Body | Moderate — deadlift focus | ~60 min |
| Sun | Rest | — | — |

> **Principle:** Small muscles (arms, lateral delts, core) recover in ~24h and are safe on consecutive days. Heavy compounds need 48-72h. When consecutive days are forced, the middle day carries the lowest CNS load.

## Session Protocol

1. **Warm-up (5-8 min):** 5 min treadmill/cycle + dynamic stretches
2. **Main lifts first.** Compounds before accessories. Rest 2 min between compound sets, 60-90s on accessories.
3. **Log everything.** Weight × reps every set.
4. **Cool-down:** 5 min walk + stretch.

---

## Progression Rules

- ✅ All reps with good form → add weight next session
- ⚠️ Miss 2+ reps → repeat same weight
- ❌ Miss reps two sessions in a row → drop 10%, rebuild
- 🛑 Sharp pain → stop, report

---

## Recovery Checklist

- [ ] 6+ hours sleep
- [ ] Calorie target with protein floor
- [ ] 2-3L water daily
- [ ] Soreness → active recovery (walk, light cycle)

## Key Rules

- **Compound-first exercise order.** Bench/Squat/Deadlift/OHP/Row before isolations.
- **Beginner = dumbbells and machines first.** Barbell only when form is demonstrably stable.
- **Progression is session-by-session, not week-by-week.** Linear for beginners. Add weight every session, not every week.
- **Rest column is mandatory in exercise tables.** Rest times by exercise type: heavy compounds (bench, squat, RDL) = 2 min. Compound-accessories (OHP, rows, pulldowns, leg press, incline, split squats) = 90 sec. Isolations (curls, laterals, face pulls, pushdowns, leg curls, calves) = 60 sec. Core (plank, dead bugs) = 30 sec.
- **Weight gates for runners.** No distances beyond 10K above 100 kg bodyweight.
