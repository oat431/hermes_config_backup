# Re-Entry & Re-Baseline Playbook

Depth for sizing a return to training after time off. Load when designing a re-entry week or a new phase after a gap, illness, or relocation.

## Detraining expectations

| Time off | Strength loss | Aerobic | Re-entry load |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 week (illness) | ~0 | small | −5%, resume after 1 clean week |
| 2–4 weeks | 5–10% | VO2max −5–10% | −10–15%, RPE-gated |
| 8–12 weeks | 10–20% | VO2max −10%+, Zone 2 pace drifts | −20–25%, full re-test |

- Muscle memory: form recall survives; rebuild is faster than a true beginner's, so ramp +2.5 kg upper / +5 kg lower per week when W1 is clean.
- RPE gate in W1: nothing above Medium. One load across all working sets.
- Cardio: re-test Zone 2 rather than resuming old HR targets; walk-first, log all three numbers (minutes, distance, avg HR).

## Kcal resume decision

| Situation | Decision |
|---|---|
| Weight flat during untrained gap | Intake ≈ sedentary maintenance. Resume at old target +150–200 kcal; adjust after 2 Sunday weigh-ins |
| Weight gained during gap | Start at old target; guardrail still applies |
| Weight lost during gap | Start +200–300 over old target; protein 160 g floor |

Guardrails: 7d MA loss >0.6 kg/wk ×2 → +200 kcal (muscle protection). Stall <0.15 kg/wk ×3 → −150 kcal or adherence audit.

## Re-baseline checklist (before a new phase after a gap or move)

1. Sunday weigh-in (smart scale, standardized protocol).
2. Schedule — availability windows from the new job.
3. Gym recon — free weights, racks, cardio, crowd at target times, hours.
4. Health — injuries, illness cleared, BP cuff present, pending labs.
5. Blood panel values (fasting glucose, HbA1c, lipids) — log vs reference ranges.
6. Tape + photos — 1st Sunday of month.
7. Food recon — where meals come from now (the stall map dies on a move; the dish-table system survives).
