# Illness / Skipped-Week Re-Entry Protocol

Worked example: W9 of Phase 3 lost to flu-A (respiratory, breathing-affected); protocol applied 2026-08-31. Reuse for any illness-lost week.

## Philosophy (say this to the user)

A sick week is a data point, not a failure — "no apology needed, and no revenge training." You do not catch up a sick week; you rebuild toward it. **No make-up volume**: no extra sets, no double sessions, no bonus running.

## Re-entry weights — the −5% rule

After ≥2 weeks off (illness + preceding break), start barbell lifts at **−5% of the last EARNED finals**, then +2.5 kg/week back to them:

| Lift | W8 earned | W10 re-entry | W11 | W12 |
|------|-----------|--------------|-----|-----|
| Bench | 45 | 42.5 | 45 | 47.5 attempt (only if W11 clean) |
| OHP | 35 | 32.5 | 35 | 35 |
| Squat | 42.5 clean | 40 | 42.5 | 45 |
| Deadlift | 70 | 65 | 70 | 75 |
| Front squat | 37.5 | 35 | 37.5 | 40 |

- "Earned" excludes ramped-invalid sessions (47.5 was logged 47.5→45→42.5 — invalid; the earned final was 45).
- Review-earned-but-untested jumps (squat 45, DL 75) wait until after the rebuild.
- Accessories: hold or one notch down; machines tolerate less deload than barbell.
- Recompute warm-up ladders inline in every rewritten day note when weights change (~50/70/90% rounded to 2.5 kg).

## Gates for the re-entry week

1. **RPE gate:** every working set Medium or easier. Anything Hard = data → repeat that load next week. Never present it to the user as a failure.
2. **Breathing gate (respiratory illness):** flu/COVID hit the lungs. Chest tightness, cough fits, or shortness of breath beyond normal → stop the set, walk it off, report. The lungs decide the cardio pace.
3. **Cardio walk-first:** first week back = 30 min walk-first; light jog only if breathing is 100% AND HR ≤137 (Zone 2). Intervals return the following week only after a clean session.
4. **Sleep/energy:** if energy crashes mid-week or sleep avg <6h → drop Sunday cardio first, then Saturday's last accessory.

## Expected — don't over-react

- RHR elevated 65–75 for 1–2 weeks post-flu — recovery signal, not danger. Don't cut training on RHR alone.
- PAI/steps for the sick week are meaningless — don't flag them in the review.
- Appetite may stay suppressed: protein 160 g is the priority even when kcal lands under target. Kcal target holds; the drop-watch trigger re-arms on the return week's data.

## Bookkeeping

- **Training log:** annotate the skipped week honestly (`## Week N — SKIPPED (flu-A)`), no tables, no backfilled data. The return-week tables replace the pre-filled ones (exercise rows change shape when the program flips home↔gym).
- **Review summary:** a "SKIPPED" section listing the decisions carried into the return week (re-entry weights, rescheduled baselines, kcal hold).
- **Measurement baselines** due in the skipped week slide forward (BP baseline week W9 → W10; edit the log's schedule note, don't silently ignore it).
- **Smart-scale file:** note the missed Sunday and invite backfill if the user happened to weigh.
