## Authoring rules

- One durable concept per note; use an index/MOC to connect the series.
- Use numbered, hyphenated filenames and avoid spaces in wikilink targets.
- Explain every unit (`Ω`, `dB`, `Hz`, `mW`, `Vrms`) on first use.
- Distinguish technical performance from tuning preference.
- Include a “what it does not prove” column to prevent marketing claims from becoming conclusions.
- Tie examples to the user's actual chain, but do not make the concept note dependent on one transient session.
- Prefer a cheap test, listening comparison, or existing setting before a purchase recommendation.
